Notes

I counted kinds of official boundary for the video. of them have a file I could put on a map. The other don't, and each one says why.

Who drew it, not the chapter. The video counts boundaries chapter by chapter, and two of those chapters are mixed. This page groups each line by who actually drew it. Switch to "Video chapter" to see them in the order I counted them, with the counter.

New York only. of the mapped types are mapped for New York City only, because that's where the building is. Some have national files I didn't need for the video. Others, like police precincts, only exist city by city.

Missing means missing. I don't draw a placeholder for a type I don't have a file for. No file, no shape.

At the building. Each mapped type says whether its line contains the Empire State Building, tested against the same file you see on the map.
